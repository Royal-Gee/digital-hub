OUR CONTEXT

Digital literacy in Zimbabwe.

Digital skills are relevant to learners, families, communities, businesses, and public services. The goal is to use technology with knowledge, care, and purpose.

Digital literacy in everyday life

Digital skills touch many aspects of daily life. Here is how they make a difference.

Mobile communication

Mobile phones are a primary way people stay connected. Knowing how to use them safely and effectively matters for everyone.

Mobile money

Digital financial services allow people to send money, pay bills, and save securely. Understanding these tools includes protecting your PIN.

Schools & learning

Digital tools support research, collaboration, e-learning, and access to educational resources beyond the classroom.

Small businesses

Digital tools help small businesses with marketing, customer communication, record keeping, inventory, and sales.

Agriculture

Weather information, market prices, and knowledge resources reached through digital platforms can support farming communities.

Online employment

Digital platforms create new opportunities for freelance work, remote employment, and skills-based income.

Communities and safety

Digital literacy strengthens communities by supporting informed, safe participation.

Cybersecurity awareness

Be alert to phishing, impersonation, scams, unsafe links, and requests for authentication details. Everyone benefits from vigilance.

Social media

Social platforms connect communities. Using them responsibly means sharing accurately, respecting others, and protecting privacy.

TOGETHER

Building a digitally literate society.

Digital literacy benefits individuals, strengthens families, empowers businesses, and supports communities.
