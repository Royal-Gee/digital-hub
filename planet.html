LITERACY FOR PLANET

Use information to make better environmental decisions.

Digital literacy can help communities find reliable environmental information, compare claims, communicate responsibly, and discover practical ways to reduce waste and protect resources.

Digital tools for environmental awareness

Technology gives us powerful ways to understand and protect the environment.

Find reliable information

Look for identifiable sources, dates, evidence, and data from relevant institutions, researchers, or environmental experts when researching climate and conservation topics.

Learn about sustainability

Digital resources explain conservation, waste reduction, climate information, renewable energy, and sustainable use of natural resources for all ages.

Share responsibly

Accurate environmental information helps communities understand issues. Always verify before sharing — misleading claims can cause unnecessary fear or inaction.

Technology and sustainability

Digital solutions are increasingly part of environmental protection. Understanding these connections helps you make more sustainable choices.

Energy use

Being mindful of device usage, turning off unused equipment, and adjusting settings can reduce energy consumption significantly over time.

E-waste

Responsible disposal and recycling of electronic devices reduces pollution and recovers valuable materials. Check local recycling options in your area.

Sustainable agriculture

Digital tools support precision farming, soil monitoring, water management, and crop planning that reduce waste and improve yields.

Smart planning

Navigation, logistics, and scheduling tools can reduce unnecessary travel and fuel use, contributing to lower emissions.
