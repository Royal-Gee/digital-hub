Online Scams

Recognise common scam patterns:

Fake giveaways: "Claim your prize" messages that ask for personal details.

Impersonation: Someone pretending to be a trusted person or institution.

Suspicious links: Links that lead to fake login pages or download malware.

Fake job offers: Offers requiring upfront payment or personal documents.

Account recovery scams: Messages claiming your account will be deleted unless you act now.

Payment scams: Unusual requests to send money via gift cards or wire transfer.

Quick safety checklist

Apply these practices every day.

Daily habits

Lock your devices, keep software updated, log out of shared computers, and be cautious with public Wi-Fi. Small consistent habits build strong security.

Think before sharing

Consider who can see what you post, how it might be used, and whether it should be shared at all. Your information has value — protect it.
