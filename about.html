THE FOUNDATION

What does it mean to be digitally literate?

It means having the knowledge, skills, and judgement to use digital technologies effectively, safely, responsibly, and creatively.

About Digital Literacy Hub

Digital Literacy Hub is a free public educational platform designed to help people of all ages build practical digital skills. Our mission is to make digital literacy accessible, engaging, and relevant to everyday life.

We believe that understanding technology is no longer optional — it shapes how we learn, work, communicate, stay safe, and create opportunity. Our content is designed to be useful whether you are just getting started or looking to strengthen existing skills.

THE PROJECT

Why the Hub exists

Using technology every day is not the same as understanding how to use it safely, critically and productively.

The problem

Digital information can be difficult to evaluate, online risks can be easy to miss, and new technologies such as AI create new questions about privacy, trust and responsible use.

The aim

The Hub provides accessible learning, realistic scenarios and immediate feedback so learners can build practical digital judgement.

The approach

Learn → Practise → Test → Improve → Apply. Visitors can follow this cycle independently without needing someone to explain the platform.

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants to understand and use technology more effectively.

Students

Build skills for research, collaboration, presentations, and future careers.

Teachers & Parents

Resources for guiding young people and integrating digital skills into learning.

Adults & Professionals

Strengthen workplace skills, explore career changes, and stay current with technology.

What we cover

Our topics span the full range of practical digital literacy.

Core topics

Online safety, information literacy, digital communication, cybersecurity, AI literacy, digital finance, and digital citizenship.

Practical areas

Career exploration, entrepreneurship, environmental responsibility, accessibility, and lifelong learning strategies.
