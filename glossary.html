Digital Literacy Glossary

Search simple explanations for important terms used across the Hub.

Phishing

A deceptive message or website designed to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.

Digital footprint

The information and traces created by a person's online activities.

Two-factor authentication

A security method that requires two different forms of verification to access an account.

Misinformation

False or inaccurate information shared without necessarily intending to deceive.

Disinformation

False or misleading information deliberately created or shared to deceive.

Malware

Software designed to damage, disrupt, spy on or gain unauthorised access to systems.

Generative AI

AI systems that can create content such as text, images, audio or code from instructions.

AI hallucination

An AI-generated response that presents incorrect or invented information as if it were true.

Privacy

The ability to control how personal information is collected, used and shared.

Cybersecurity

Practices and technologies used to protect devices, systems, accounts and information.

E-waste

Discarded electronic devices and components that require responsible reuse, recycling or disposal.

Digital citizenship

Responsible, safe, respectful and informed participation in digital life.
