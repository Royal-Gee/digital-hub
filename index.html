Three dimensions of digital literacy

Digital literacy touches every part of modern life. Understanding these dimensions helps you use technology with purpose and confidence.

People

Digital literacy helps people communicate, learn, stay safe, and participate in society. It includes how we treat others online, manage our identity, and support inclusion.

Planet

Digital technology can help us understand environmental issues, make sustainable choices, and reduce our impact through informed, responsible use of devices and data.

Why digital literacy matters

Every day, people make decisions online that affect their opportunities, safety, and wellbeing. Digital literacy helps you make better ones.

Protect yourself

Knowing how passwords, phishing, privacy settings, and safe browsing work helps you avoid scams and protect personal information. Small habits make a big difference.

Make informed decisions

From news to health advice to financial offers, digital literacy teaches you to evaluate sources, check evidence, and think critically about what you find online.

Build opportunity

Digital skills open doors to education, employment, and entrepreneurship. From applying for jobs to starting a business, technology creates new pathways to success.

Stay connected

Meaningful participation in a connected world requires knowing how to communicate respectfully, share responsibly, and support inclusive digital communities.
