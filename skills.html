SKILLS MAP

Build skills for a changing world.

Digital literacy is a group of connected skills. You do not need to master everything at once — keep learning and applying what you know.

01

Computer Literacy

Files, folders, applications, documents, presentations, and spreadsheets.

Beginner: Practise organising files and creating documents
02

Internet Literacy

Search, websites, browsers, online services, and safe downloads.

Beginner: Learn to evaluate website credibility
03

Information Literacy

Sources, evidence, comparison, context, and recognising misinformation.

Beginner: Check source and date before sharing
04

Cybersecurity

Passwords, phishing awareness, privacy settings, and safe browsing.

Beginner: Use strong unique passwords
05

Digital Communication

Email, messaging, social media etiquette, and professional collaboration.

Beginner: Practise clear, respectful online messaging
06

Digital Creativity

Design, photo and video editing, content creation, and coding.

Beginner: Try free design or coding tools
07

Digital Financial Literacy

Online banking, mobile payments, budgeting apps, and fraud awareness.

Beginner: Understand your bank's security features
08

AI Literacy

Understanding generative AI, its capabilities, limitations, and responsible use.

Beginner: Experiment with AI tools critically
09

Problem Solving

Using digital tools to analyse problems, find solutions, and make data-informed decisions.

Beginner: Break problems into steps, then search for solutions
10

Digital Citizenship

Respectful online behaviour, understanding your rights, and participating positively.

Beginner: Review privacy settings on all accounts
