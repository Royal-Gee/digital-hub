Resources for the next step.

Explore topics and skills further with these educational categories. Search for trusted learning materials on each subject.

Browse by topic

Each category connects to areas of digital literacy you can explore and practise.

Digital Skills

Build foundational and advanced skills in computing, internet use, productivity software, coding, design, and communication.

Online Safety

Learn about passwords, phishing, privacy, device updates, and safe online behaviour from established cybersecurity educators.

Information Literacy

Practise source evaluation, lateral reading, evidence checking, and recognising misleading presentation techniques.

AI Literacy

Explore how artificial intelligence works, its capabilities and limitations, and how to use AI tools responsibly.

Digital Finance

Understand online banking, mobile payments, budgeting tools, and how to protect financial information.

Digital Careers

Discover career paths in technology, design, data, marketing, and entrepreneurship that use digital skills.

Environment

Learn about sustainable digital practices, e-waste, energy conservation, and technology's environmental impact.

Learning

Find platforms and methods for continuous learning — courses, tutorials, practice projects, and communities.

General learning platforms

Search for free and paid courses in your area of interest.

Online course platforms

Major platforms offer courses in technology, business, creative skills, and more. Look for courses with good reviews and structured content.

Free coding resources

Several organisations provide free coding tutorials and practice environments for beginners of all ages.

Public libraries

Many libraries offer free internet access, computers, and digital literacy workshops. Check your local library for programmes.
