AI LITERACY

Understand the intelligence behind the tools.

Artificial intelligence is transforming how we work, learn, and solve problems. Understanding what AI can and cannot do is essential for responsible use.

What is AI?

Artificial intelligence refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence — such as understanding language, recognising images, and making predictions.

Generative AI

A type of AI that creates text, images, code, and other content. It learns patterns from large datasets and generates new outputs based on those patterns.

How AI helps

AI assists with education (personalised learning), business (automation and analysis), healthcare (diagnostics), and environmental work (climate modelling).

AI limitations

AI is powerful but imperfect. Understanding its limits helps you use it wisely.

Hallucinations

AI can confidently produce information that sounds correct but is entirely false. Always verify important facts.

Bias

AI reflects the data it was trained on. Results can contain unfair biases related to gender, race, or culture.

Privacy

Using AI tools may involve sharing data. Be cautious about entering personal or sensitive information into AI services.

No understanding

AI does not truly understand meaning. It processes patterns. Human judgement remains essential for context and ethics.

Use AI as a tool, not an authority

Treat AI output as a starting point. Always cross-check important claims against reliable sources before acting on them.

Protect your privacy

Never share passwords, financial details, or sensitive personal information with AI tools. Understand each tool's data policy.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Think critically about AI.

AI can be a powerful assistant when used wisely. Stay curious, stay cautious, and always verify.
